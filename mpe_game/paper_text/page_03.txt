Remark 2: The swap threshold ϱ is crucial for ensuring sig-
niﬁcant beneﬁts from target switches by pursuers. Increasing ϱ
raises the switching barrier, potentially reducing the frequency
or possibility of switches. Decreasing ϱ makes switching easier
and more frequent, but excessive switching for trivial gains is
impractical in engineering. The simulation section will carefully
determine an appropriate ϱ value.
