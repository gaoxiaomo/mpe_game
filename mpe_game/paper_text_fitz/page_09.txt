ear aircraft system is presented to validate the effectiveness
of the proposed off-policy RL control algorithm. Here, we
present two simulation scenarios: multiple-pursuer-one-evader
and multiple-pursuer-multiple-evader. In addition, we analyze
the impact of saturation constraints, conduct comparative ex-
periments on cohesion, and assess computational burden.
